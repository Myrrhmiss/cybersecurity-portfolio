# Course 2: Security Domains, Risk, and Monitoring

## Module 1: CISSP Security Domains & Risk Fundamentals
- Explored the **eight CISSP security domains** (Part 1 & Part 2) relevant to cybersecurity analysts.
- Identified **threats, risks, and vulnerabilities** affecting information systems.
- Analyzed the **key impacts** of threats, risks, and vulnerabilities on organizations.
- Introduced the **NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)** as a structured approach to managing security risk.
- Learned how to **identify and manage common threats, risks, and vulnerabilities**.

---

## Module 2: Security Audits & OWASP Principles
- Studied **OWASP security principles** and their role in application and system security.
- Learned how to **plan a security audit**, including scope, objectives, and controls.
- Practiced how to **complete a security audit** and document findings.

---

## Module 3: Security Monitoring & SIEM Tools
- Explored **common SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) tools**.
- Learned how to **use SIEM tools to monitor, detect, and respond to security events**.
- Examined how SIEM supports organizational security operations and incident response.

---

## Module 4: Professional Collaboration
- Emphasized the importance of **connecting and collaborating with peers** in cybersecurity.
- Explored professional communication, teamwork, and knowledge sharing within security environments.
