# Course 1: Foundations of Cybersecurity


## Module 1: Core Skills & the Importance of Cybersecurity
- Identified **core responsibilities of cybersecurity professionals**, including threat prevention, detection, and response.
- Explored **transferable skills** (critical thinking, communication, problem-solving) and **technical skills** (risk assessment, security principles, basic networking).
- Examined the **importance of cybersecurity** in protecting systems, data, people, and organizations in a digital world.

---

## Module 2: Security Domains, Attacks, and Adversaries
- Introduced the **eight CISSP security domains** (Part 1 & Part 2) to understand the broad landscape of cybersecurity.
- Learned how to **identify and categorize different types of cyber attacks**.
- Analyzed **attacker motivations, behaviors, and methods** to better understand threat actors.

---

## Module 3: Ethics in Cybersecurity
- Studied **ethical principles** relevant to cybersecurity decision-making.
- Explored how ethics guide responsible behavior, trust, and accountability in security roles.
- Applied ethical frameworks to real-world cybersecurity scenarios.

---

## Module 4: Building a Cybersecurity Portfolio
- Learned how to **create and structure a cybersecurity portfolio**.
- Identified key artifacts to demonstrate skills, learning progress, and professional readiness.
- Emphasized documentation, clarity, and continuous learning as part of career development.
