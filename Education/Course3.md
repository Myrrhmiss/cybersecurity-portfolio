# Course 3: Connect and Protect Networks and Network Security

## Module 1: Networking Fundamentals
- Defined **what networks are** and their role in modern organizations.
- Explored **network tools** and their functions.
- Identified **network components, devices, and diagrams** used to design and document networks.
- Studied **cloud networks**, **cloud computing**, and **software-defined networks (SDN)**.
- Identified the **functions of common network tools**.

---

## Module 2: Network Communication Models
- Introduced **network communication concepts**.
- Studied the **TCP/IP model** and its purpose.
- Identified the **four layers of the TCP/IP model**.
- Explored the **OSI model** and its relationship to TCP/IP.
- Examined **IP addresses** and their role in network communication.
- Identified key **components of network-layer communication**.

---

## Module 3: Subnetting and Network Design
- Learned **subnetting concepts** and their purpose in network segmentation.
- Studied **CIDR (Classless Inter-Domain Routing)** and efficient IP address allocation.

---

## Module 4: Network Privacy and Protection Technologies
- Explored **proxy servers** and their role in privacy and security.
- Studied **virtual networks** and privacy considerations.
- Learned about **VPN protocols**, including:
  - WireGuard
  - IPsec (Internet Protocol Security)
- Examined how VPNs protect data in transit.

---

## Module 5: Network Attacks and Interception
- Reviewed **common interception tactics**.
- Identified **network-based attacks**.
- Analyzed how network attacks are executed and detected.

---

## Module 6: Reflection and Professional Connection
- Reflected on networking and security concepts.
- Emphasized **connecting and collaborating with peers** in cybersecurity environments.
